Spotifin privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Spotifin is a music player for your own Jellyfin server. The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Spotifin stores your server address, your sign-in token, your settings and downloaded songs only on your device. You can delete them at any time by signing out or deleting the app.

Who the app talks to

  • The Jellyfin server you add, to show and play your music.
  • A Downtify server, only if you add one in the settings.
  • LRCLIB (lrclib.net), to get synced lyrics for the song that plays. It receives the song title, artist, album and duration.

Spotifin has no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
